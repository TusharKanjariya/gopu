# Gopu — Brand Kit

**Product name:** Gopu  
**Display name:** gopu.  
**Category:** Open-source, privacy-first personal AI memory companion  
**Tagline:** Your little buddy with a big memory.  
**Short tagline:** Your memory. Your machine.  
**Brand personality:** Friendly, calm, warm, trustworthy, playful, minimal.  

## One-line pitch

Gopu is a friendly, open-source desktop AI companion that helps you remember your digital work and find useful context while putting you in control of your data.

## Short description

Gopu is your little AI buddy with a big memory. Designed to run locally and respect your privacy, Gopu helps you remember what you've read, written, and worked on, so you can pick up where you left off without explaining everything again.

## Longer product description

Meet Gopu: a friendly, open-source AI memory companion for your desktop. Gopu is designed to help you keep track of your work across apps, recall useful context, and get help with everyday writing and productivity. Privacy is central to its design: local-first storage, transparent code, explicit controls over what gets captured, and an architecture intended to support local AI models. Gopu aims to feel less like another chatbot and more like a little buddy who remembers the important things.

**Important:** The descriptions above communicate the product vision. Do not claim specific features or that all processing is fully offline until the implementation is built and verified.

## Suggested README introduction

# 🐻 Gopu

**Your little buddy with a big memory.**

Gopu is an open-source, privacy-first desktop AI memory companion. Its goal is to help you remember useful context from your work and make everyday tasks easier, while giving you clear control over your information.

### What we're building

- **Personal memory:** Bring useful desktop context together in one place.
- **Contextual assistance:** Ask questions and get help using the information you've chosen to keep.
- **Local-first design:** Store memory on your device by default.
- **Transparent privacy controls:** Choose which apps and content Gopu can access.
- **Open-source foundation:** Understand, modify, and improve the software yourself.

> Project status: Early development. Features listed above are planned goals unless marked as implemented.
