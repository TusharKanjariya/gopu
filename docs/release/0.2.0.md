Gopu 0.2.0 adds **reminders**, for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). If you have 0.1.0, it offers this update by itself.

- **Reminders.** Settings → Reminders: a title, a day and time, and how early to show it. When it's due, a note slides
  out of the Ask Gopu tab with **Snooze** and **Done**. If your PC was asleep, it shows when you're back, marked
  "missed".
- **Just say it.** Type "remind me to call Anna at 5, 10 min early" in the Ask box, in any language, and Gopu shows
  what it read with **Add** and **Change**. Nothing is saved until you click Add.
- **Remind you?** When a reply you write with the Gopu key sets a plan ("I'll send it by Friday"), the tab offers a
  reminder for 15 seconds. It's read by the same model call that writes the reply, so it costs nothing extra. Turn it
  off on the Reminders page.
- **Prep me.** On a due reminder, Gopu tells you what memory knows about it: the person, what was said last, what you
  promised, with links to where you saw it.
- **Check-ins.** Any saved ask, such as your daily report, on the days and time you pick. 0.1.0's end-of-day reminder
  becomes your first check-in.

Also: settings are saved in one step, and an older Gopu no longer drops settings it doesn't know.

## Install
1. Download `Gopu_0.2.0_x64-setup.exe` below.
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**. This preview isn't code-signed yet.
   To check the file is ours, run `Get-FileHash .\Gopu_0.2.0_x64-setup.exe` in PowerShell and compare the hash with
   `SHA256SUMS.txt` below (upper or lower case doesn't matter).

## Known issues
- A reminder asked for in a language other than English ("yaad karavje") needs memory on; "remind me…" works either way.
- Text written for another AI or a search box can be taken as an instruction when it was meant as a draft, or the
  other way round.
- Some windows are captured with their title only.
- People pages don't cover Teams yet, and read WhatsApp status words in English only.
- The Gopu key can't be turned off for a single app yet.
- No macOS or Windows on ARM yet.

Settings and memory formats may change between 0.x versions.
