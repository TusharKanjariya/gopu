Gopu's first preview, for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

**Tap the Gopu key (Right Ctrl) in any text box.** An empty box gets a reply drafted from what's on screen, a draft gets
polished in your style, an instruction gets carried out, and a selection gets rewritten. **Tap outside a text box to
ask your memory**, such as "what did I do today?", or a saved ask like your daily report.

- **Open source** (Apache-2.0), so you can check what an app that reads your screen does.
- **Your model, your key.** Groq's free tier by default, or OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, or Ollama on your own PC.
- **Nothing in the background.** No account and no analytics. The only request Gopu makes by itself is the daily update
  check, and you can turn it off.

## Install
1. Download `Gopu_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` below.
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**. This preview isn't code-signed yet.
   To check the file is ours, run `Get-FileHash .\Gopu_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` in PowerShell and compare the hash with
   `SHA256SUMS.txt` below (upper or lower case doesn't matter).
3. The welcome walks you through a free Groq key and your first tap.

## Known issues
- Text written for another AI or a search box can be taken as an instruction when it was meant as a draft, or the
  other way round.
- Some windows are captured with their title only.
- People pages don't cover Teams yet, and read WhatsApp status words in English only.
- The Gopu key can't be turned off for a single app yet.
- No macOS or Windows on ARM yet.

Settings and memory formats may change between 0.x versions.
