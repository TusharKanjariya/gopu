Saved asks

The ★ buttons in the Ask Gopu box. Save one with "Save as ask" under any answer, for example your daily work report in your own format.

Updates

The check asks github.com whether a newer Gopu exists; nothing about you or your memory goes with it. Nothing installs until you choose to.

Logs

Saves what Gopu read and wrote on your last tap, so a bug can be reported. It includes text from your screen, so leave it off unless you need it. Turning it off deletes the log.
