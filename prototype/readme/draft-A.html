gopu.

Your little buddy with a big memory.
Remembers what you saw. Writes what you mean.

[ demo: a tap of the Gopu key, Gopu walks along the field and writes the reply ]

Tap one key in any text box

The box has… Gopu…
nothing drafts a reply from what's on screen
your draft polishes it, in your style
an instruction ("make this shorter", "say no politely") carries it out
a selection rewrites just that part

It works in mail, chat, browsers and documents, in the app you're already in.

Ask your memory anything

Gopu reads on-screen text through Windows accessibility (never screenshots) into a local, searchable memory. Tap the key outside a text box and ask: "what did I do today?", "what's unfinished?", or a saved ask like your daily work report, in your own format. Every answer links to what it came from.

Open, free and yours

  • Open source, so you can check what an app that reads your screen does.
  • Bring any model: Groq's free tier, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, or Ollama on your own PC.
  • Nothing in the background: no account, no analytics, no model calls you didn't ask for.

Install

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). No admin rights needed.

  1. Download Gopu_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe from the latest release.
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click More info → Run anyway. This preview isn't code-signed yet; to check the download is ours, compare Get-FileHash .\Gopu_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe with SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page.
  3. The welcome asks for a model key. A free Groq key takes a minute, or pick Ollama to keep everything on your PC.
  4. Click into any text box and tap Right Ctrl.

Gopu starts with Windows and updates itself when you say so. Uninstalling keeps your memory unless you tick the box to delete it.

Privacy

Stays on your PC Leaves your PC
Everything memory captures, in one local file A Gopu-key tap or a question: sent to your model, only when you ask
Your people pages, style rules and chats The daily update check, which carries nothing about you
Your model key, in the Windows credential store Nothing else. No account, no analytics

Memory never reads passwords, sign-in or payment pages, banks, health sites, password managers or private windows. Card numbers, keys and ID numbers are masked before anything is saved. Pause it, add apps to "Never capture", or turn it off in Settings → Privacy.

Use your memory in other AI apps

Turn on Settings → Privacy → Let other AI apps read my memory, then Copy setup for Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor or any MCP client. It's read-only and masked, and nothing is added to other apps for you.

Build from source

cargo test -p gopu-core
cargo build -p gopu-desktop            # Gopu.exe and gopu-mcp.exe
cd apps/desktop && npx @tauri-apps/cli@2 build   # the installer
